Ancient Greek Ceramics

Greek architecture and sculpture is revered around the world. But, Greek artisans also had great prowess in the medium of pottery.

Renaissance Mosaic

Mosaic art is a type of art that creates a design by using small pieces of material, such as glass, stone or even recycled materials. The pieces are called tesserae, and they are typically arranged in a grid pattern. Mosaics can be used to decorate a variety of surfaces, including walls, floors, ceilings, and pools.

Post Modern Art

Post modern art is characterized by its rejection of traditional artistic values and its embrace of abstract stylings. Our display features several paintings and exhibitions by acclaimed contemporary artists.
